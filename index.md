## What we work on

PieTeams is an independent open-source team. We focus on one problem: **how organisations keep knowledge and master data usable over time**.

Most software stores data. Far less software makes that data understandable — to new employees, to downstream systems and to AI agents. Our projects approach the same problem from three angles:

| Project | Question it answers | Primary users |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **PieKBS** | How do we turn scattered documents into knowledge an agent can search and cite? | AI engineers, researchers, technical teams |
| **PieMDM** | How do we keep customers, suppliers, products and other master data consistent? | Data stewards, enterprise IT, operations |
| **PiePLM** | How can smaller manufacturers manage product information without a heavyweight PLM suite? | Manufacturing SMEs, product teams |

## How we build

- **Explicit over implicit.** A Markdown page you can review beats a vector you cannot explain.
- **Small over sprawling.** One binary or one focused platform beats a suite of half-used modules.
- **Standards over lock-in.** SQL, HTTP, OpenAPI and MCP are interfaces we can all inspect.
- **Documentation as a deliverable.** If a feature cannot be explained, it is not finished.
